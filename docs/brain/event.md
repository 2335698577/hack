## Places and kit

- **Hardware library:** top floor of the TLC. Trade your student ID to borrow. Queue to borrow or return. Remember your table number. RS hardware (Arduino UNO Q, cameras, sensors) is lent through the RS Product Loan Library.
- **3D printer:** see the `#3d-print-info` Discord channel.
- **Sleeping space:** TLC123.
- **HackPacks:** beginner guides on GitHub, web dev and machine learning (QR code on the slides).
- **Help:** help desk, volunteers and organisers in DurHack t-shirts, or Discord.

## Safety

- Fire alarm: leave by the nearest fire exit (green running-person signs). Assembly point is just outside the front of the building. No drills are planned.
- Injury: find an organiser. Emergency: call 999. Building postcode **DH1 3LS**.

## Main judging criteria

DurHack judges every project on:

1. Creativity and innovation
2. Technical competence and execution
3. Research and learning
4. Polish and completion

Overall prizes: 1st place, 2nd place, and the Spirit of the Dino award.

## Rules worth remembering

- Maximum four people per team.
- From our handbook: all project code and designs must be made after 12:00 Saturday. On Devpost, say honestly what was pre-existing (Bit:Bot chassis, micro:bit, open-source libraries).
- Organisers asked us to take breaks and get some sleep.
