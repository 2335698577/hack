NEPTUNE NORTH CHALLENGE
Challenge background
Modern technology often relies on multiple systems collaborating on a common goal. For example, drones in a light show; robots in fulfilment centres; or LLMs solving different parts of a large problem.
In some situations, communication between these systems is unreliable or unavailable. When this happens, each actor needs to be able to operate without receiving instructions from a central hub.
We want you to design an approach to a problem which involves systems working together in a distributed fashion, even when communications are interrupted.
